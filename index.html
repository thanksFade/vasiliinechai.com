Arcavo

Warehouse accounting for 3PL operators

2026 · Product, design & build · Next.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL

3PL operators — warehouses that hold other companies’ goods — get paid by the pallet-day. Most small ones still run that on spreadsheets: receipts on paper, monthly statements rebuilt by hand, statutory forms typed from scratch. Arcavo replaces the whole loop with one system.

I designed and built it end to end with AI tooling — product, data model, security, infrastructure and deployment. It has been in production at a cold-storage operator since June 2026.

Under the hood

  • Stock is tracked by batch: every dispatch draws from a specific receipt, and the database itself refuses any change that would break the balance. A nightly reconciliation alerts on the slightest drift.
  • Multi-tenant: row-level security in the database isolates each organisation’s data, and the access rules are covered by automated tests.
  • A full audit trail of who changed what, readable by people rather than engineers.
  • Self-hosted on a VPS in Russia, with encrypted off-site backups and restores tested end to end.
